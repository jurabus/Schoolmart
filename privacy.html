Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information SchoolMart collects when you use our website (schoolmart.pro) and our mobile app, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account or place an order we collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your phone number (used as your login)
  • Your delivery address
  • A password, which is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; we can never see it
  • The products, quantities and prices in your orders, the supplier you ordered from, and the dates

To protect the service from abuse, our servers also briefly record the network (IP) address that places orders or looks them up. This is used only for rate limiting and is deleted within one day.

We do not collect payment card details, precise location, contacts, or any advertising identifiers. Suppliers who use our app may upload photos of their products; customers' photos are never accessed.

How we use it

  • To prepare, confirm and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order
  • To let you look up your order using your phone number and order code

Who can see your order

SchoolMart is a marketplace of school suppliers. When you order, the supplier you ordered from can see that order, including your name, phone number and delivery address, so they can prepare and deliver it. Suppliers cannot see your other orders or your account.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the suppliers you order from and the service providers that run our systems.

Where it is stored

Order data is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the European Union. An order can be viewed only by you (signed in, or with your phone number and order code), the supplier you ordered from, and SchoolMart staff through a password-protected administration panel.

The app and website also save your cart and the codes of orders you placed on your own device, so you can find them again. You can remove these by deleting them in the app, clearing your browser data, or uninstalling the app.

Tracking and analytics

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil orders and meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which they are deleted.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can view and edit your name and address in the app at any time (Account → Edit profile). You can permanently delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account). This removes your login and profile immediately. Past orders remain with the supplier for their delivery and accounting records, no longer linked to an account.

You can also ask us to show, correct or delete information connected to your orders by contacting us.

Children

Our service is intended for parents and guardians placing orders. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
