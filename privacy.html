Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information SchoolMart collects when you use our website (schoolmart.pro) and our mobile app, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you place an order we collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery address
  • The products, quantities and prices in your order, and the date it was placed

To protect the service from abuse, our servers also briefly record the network (IP) address that places orders or looks them up. This is used only for rate limiting and is deleted within one day.

We do not require an account, and we do not collect payment card details, precise location, contacts, photos, or any advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To prepare, confirm and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order
  • To let you look up your order using your phone number and order code

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the service providers that run our systems.

Where it is stored

Order data is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the European Union. Access is restricted to our staff through a password-protected administration panel. An order can only be viewed by someone who knows both the phone number and the unique order code.

The app and website also save your cart and the codes of orders you placed on your own device, so you can find them again. You can remove these by deleting them in the app, clearing your browser data, or uninstalling the app.

Tracking and analytics

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil orders and meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which they are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information connected to your orders by contacting us with your phone number and order code.

Children

Our service is intended for parents and guardians placing orders. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
